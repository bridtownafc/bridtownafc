# Commercial Architecture

## Objective

Commercial content is a managed product catalogue and partner storytelling platform, not a static logo wall. It must help businesses understand value, find a relevant opportunity and submit a structured enquiry.

## Model

- **Opportunity:** name, category, description, price/range/POA, VAT note, duration, availability window, benefits, imagery, featured state, CTA and linked inventory.
- **Inventory:** the sponsorable asset, capacity, season/period, location, status and reservations.
- **Partner:** company profile, approved logo, link, tier, sponsored asset, dates, story/article relationships and display rules.
- **Campaign/placement:** where and when a partner appears across homepage, match, player, event or article surfaces.
- **Enquiry:** category, opportunity, organisation, contact, message, consent, source page, campaign metadata, status, owner and follow-up timestamps.

Existing examples—£500 annual perimeter board, £450 match sponsor with meal for 10 and ball sponsorship, £350 match sponsor without meal, and £100 ball sponsor—are seed content only. Prices and benefits stay CMS-managed.

## User journey

Commercial landing -> choose objective or opportunity -> view tangible benefits and availability -> submit contextual enquiry -> confirmation and response expectation. High-value principal, stadium and shirt partnerships lead to a conversation rather than a checkout.

## Sponsor value

- Meaningful tier and asset placement.
- Linked company profile and partnership story.
- Match, player or event attribution where relevant.
- Contract-aware start/expiry scheduling.
- Future analytics using agreed definitions for impressions, clicks and referrals, with bot filtering and privacy controls.

## Enquiry workflow

Statuses: new, acknowledged, qualified, proposal, won, lost, archived. Initially, notification may be email-backed, but a durable record must exist before success is shown. Future CRM sync is handled by an adapter and must not make the website dependent on one CRM.

## Governance

- Commercial editors manage packages and partners; pricing publication can require approval.
- Never expose private contract value, notes or contact history publicly.
- Logo use follows partner brand permissions.
- Expiry alerts at configurable intervals prevent stale sponsor promotion.
- Inventory prevents accidental double-selling of exclusive assets.
