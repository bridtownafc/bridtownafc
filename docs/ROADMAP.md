# Development Roadmap

## 1. Discovery and Architecture — current

Confirm boundaries, data/content models, CMS direction, match-data strategy, design direction, migration approach, security and roadmap.

## 2. Application Foundation

Approve stack and CMS after an editor usability/cost trial; initialise application, code quality, environments, typed data layer and preview workflow.

## 3. Design System and Navigation

Ingest official crest and photography, confirm red/font tokens, build accessible components and mobile navigation.

## 4. Homepage Experience

Deliver next match/latest result, league position, news, matchday actions, community and commercial placements.

## 5. Match Centre

Fixtures, results, match details, filtering, table snapshots and data provenance. Define operating procedure before automation.

## 6. Squad and Player Profiles

Teams, players, staff, season statistics and player sponsors.

## 7. News and Content

Editorial workflow, article types, related content, media rules and structured data.

## 8. Commercial Platform

Opportunities, inventory, pricing/POA, benefits and structured enquiries.

## 9. Sponsors and Partners

Partner directory, tiers, stories, scheduled placements and expiry management.

## 10. Club, Stadium and Community

History, directions, officials, honours, facilities, policies, safeguarding, accessibility and events.

## 11. CMS and Administration

Roles, approvals, audit history, editor guidance, backups and operational ownership.

## 12. SEO, Accessibility and Performance

Metadata, schema, sitemaps, WCAG review, Core Web Vitals and low-connectivity testing.

## 13. Data and Content Migration

Full URL/content inventory, redirects, media rights, cleanup and rehearsed migration.

## 14. QA and Production Readiness

Cross-device QA, security, forms, data accuracy, analytics/consent, training, monitoring and rollback rehearsal.

## 15. Controlled Migration and Launch

Approved cutover, redirects, indexing, monitoring and hypercare. Existing WordPress remains available for rollback until acceptance.

## Later capabilities

Ticketing, season tickets, shop, hospitality booking, membership/accounts, subscriptions/push, live match updates, richer statistics, CRM, sponsor analytics, video/streaming, more teams, PWA and native app are separate roadmap decisions rather than hidden Phase 1 commitments.
